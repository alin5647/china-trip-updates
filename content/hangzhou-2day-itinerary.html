📅 Day 1: West Lake Sunset Itinerary (Revised Final Plan)

13:00–14:30 Yunqi Bamboo Path (云栖竹径)

Cultural & Historical Context: Yunqi Bamboo Path is a secluded mountain valley on West Lake’s western fringe, a tranquil ancient scenic spot with over 1,000 years of history. It was historically a Buddhist pilgrimage route with stone trails, pavilions and dense bamboo forest. The thick canopy filters midday sunlight, creating soft dappled light. Scholars and monks historically favored this valley for quiet retreat and meditation, preserving classic Jiangnan mountain-forest aesthetics away from the crowded core lakefront.

📸 Photo Spot Callout: Winding stone paths framed by tall bamboo; Xixin Pavilion pond reflections; Huilong Pavilion framed valley views. Conserve walking energy for the evening sunset shoot.

🔗 Official Link: West Lake Scenic Area – Yunqi

14:30–15:15 Transit to West Lake North Shore (Six Park Drop-off)

Relaxed transfer back to the north lakefront. Rest and hydrate before the lakeside walking tour.

15:15–16:40 Six Park → Bai Causeway → Pinghu Qiuyue (六公园 · 白堤 · 平湖秋月)

Cultural & Historical Context: Bai Causeway was built in the Tang Dynasty by poet-governor Bai Juyi, one of West Lake’s iconic ancient lake divisions. Pinghu Qiuyue (“Autumn Moon Over Calm Lake”) is one of the famous Ten Scenes of West Lake. This northern shore has open lake views, willow trees and traditional lake pavilions. Late-afternoon pre-golden-hour soft daylight is ideal for relaxed lakeside atmosphere photography.

📸 Photo Spot Callout: Wide-angle lake views with distant hills; willow branches as natural foreground framing. Save dramatic sunset shooting for Leifeng Pagoda later.

🔗 Official Link: West Lake Scenic Area – Bai Causeway / Pinghu Qiuyue

16:40–16:44 Southbound Lakeside Walk Toward Leifeng Pagoda

Scenic pedestrian lakeside walk from north shore toward the south sunset zone, timed to arrive right as golden hour begins.

16:44–17:32 Leifeng Pagoda + Changqiao Park / Shuangtou Bridge (雷峰塔 + 长桥公园双投桥) — Golden Hour Peak Sunset

Cultural & Historical Context (Leifeng Pagoda): Leifeng Pagoda stands on Sunset Hill on West Lake’s southern bank, home to the iconic Ten Scene “Leifeng Pagoda in Evening Glow”. The original pagoda was built in 977 AD during the Wuyue Kingdom and collapsed naturally in 1924. The 2002 reconstruction preserves the original ancient pagoda foundation ruins inside its base. It is famously tied to the Legend of the White Snake, and remains Hangzhou’s most symbolic sunset landmark.

Cultural & Historical Context (Changqiao Park / Shuangtou Bridge): This historic waterside park is linked to the classic folk legend of Liang Shanbo and Zhu Yingtai’s eighteen-li farewell. It is the favorite local photographer viewpoint for Leifeng sunset, with unobstructed low shoreline framing of the pagoda silhouette and calm lake reflections.

📸 Photo Spot Callout: 16:44–17:17 warm golden light on pagoda; 17:17 sunset silhouette; 17:32–17:42 blue hour with lit pagoda. Prime fixed spot: Shuangtou Bridge waterside embankment for the classic postcard sunset composition.

🔗 Official Link: Leifeng Pagoda Official Website

17:32–18:00 Beishan Street (北山路) Dusk Twilight Blue Hour

Cultural & Historical Context: Beishan Street is a century-old historic avenue lined with Republic-era villas, plane trees, and panoramic West Lake views. After sunset it transitions to quiet blue-hour ambiance, with newly lit street lamps and distant Baoshu Pagoda silhouettes.

📸 Photo Spot Callout: S-curve tree-lined street perspective; twilight sky + streetlamp glow; distant Baoshu Pagoda silhouette.

🔗 Official Link: West Lake Scenic Area – Beishan Street

18:00+ Dinner & Return to Shangri-La

Casual local dinner near West Lake core, then return to hotel to rest.

📅 Day 2: Morning Mist + Temples + Tea Hills + Wetland + Grand Canal Blue Hour

Confirmed Rules: Complimentary Shangri-La breakfast; light snack-only lunch at Meijiawu; strict 2-hour Xixi Wetland; Gongchen Bridge = quick photos only (no full Qiaoxi historic town stroll, to avoid repeating Wuzhen scenery); luggage pickup delayed to 19:00–20:00; final late transfer to Holiday Inn Airport Zone.

06:15 Depart Shangri-La by DiDi → Maojiabu

06:30–07:30 Maojiabu Morning Lake Mist (茅家埠晨雾)

Cultural & Historical Context: Maojiabu is West Lake’s quiet western inlet, historically the ancient pilgrim incense road leading to mountain temples. It retains wild reed wetlands, shallow shallows and original village shorelines. Late-autumn early morning mist hovers over the water, creating ethereal Jiangnan scenery rarely seen in crowded central lake zones.

📸 Photo Spot Callout: Mist over reed banks; wooden ancient pier reflections; soft diffused morning light. Mist dissipates after 07:45 — finish shooting by 07:30.

🔗 Official Link: West Lake Scenic Area Portal

07:35–08:40 Return to Shangri-La for Complimentary Breakfast

08:40 Depart for Lingyin Feilai Peak

09:00–10:30 Feilai Peak + Lingyin Temple (飞来峰 + 灵隐寺)

Cultural & Historical Context: Founded in 326 AD by Indian monk Huili, Lingyin Temple is the most prestigious Zen Buddhist temple in the Jiangnan region. Adjacent Feilai Peak preserves over 380 ancient stone Buddhist grotto carvings from the Five Dynasties, Song and Yuan dynasties — the finest surviving grotto art south of the Yangtze River. The whole valley sits enclosed by forested mountains, a sacred Buddhist landscape with over 1,700 years of heritage.

📸 Photo Spot Callout: Weathered grotto Buddha carvings; symmetrical temple hall framing; mountain valley forest atmosphere. Keep a fast walking pace.

🔗 Official Link: West Lake Scenic Area Portal

10:30–11:20 Yongfu Temple (永福寺)

Cultural & Historical Context: Also founded by monk Huili, Yongfu Temple sits on a quiet hillside inside the Lingyin scenic zone, far less crowded than the main Lingyin Temple. It breaks the rigid symmetrical temple layout, weaving halls, ponds, tea terraces and stone paths naturally into the slope, blending Buddhist serenity with Jiangnan garden aesthetics and small Longjing tea plantations.

📸 Photo Spot Callout: Temple pond reflections; layered downhill tea terrace views; maple-framed archway scenic frames.

🔗 Official Link: West Lake Scenic Area Portal

11:25 DiDi to Meijiawu Tea Village

11:50–13:00 Meijiawu Tea Hills Photography + Light Snack Lunch (梅家坞)

Cultural & Historical Context: Meijiawu Valley is one of the core historic Longjing tea producing regions, with centuries of family tea-farming heritage. The broad open valley features rolling layered terraced tea fields, more natural and less commercialized than crowded Longjing Ancient Village, representing the classic pastoral tea landscape of West Lake’s mountain outskirts.

📸 Photo Spot Callout: Wide-angle layered tea terrace valley panoramas; rural country lane foregrounds; continuous green hill tea row textures. Light snack lunch only to preserve schedule buffer for Xixi Wetland.

13:15 DiDi to Xixi Wetland (Zhoujia Village Entrance)

13:40–15:40 Xixi National Wetland Park (西溪湿地) — Strict 2-Hour Visit

Cultural & Historical Context: Xixi Wetland is a rare urban natural wetland with millennial recluse cultural heritage. Dating to the Southern Song Dynasty, it earned the famous imperial remark “Xixi, let it remain”. It is a patchwork of water channels, reed marshes, wooden island bridges and ancient village relics, representing Jiangnan water-town wetland civilization distinct from West Lake mountain-lake scenery.

📸 Photo Spot Callout: Use official electric hop-on-hop-off sightseeing boats only; focus on Shentankou and Yanshui Yuzhuang core docks; capture water lane reflections, reed marshes, and water hamlet architecture. Strict 2-hour time limit.

🔗 Official Link: Xixi Wetland Official Website

15:45–17:30 Transit to Gongchen Bridge Grand Canal

17:30–18:10 Gongchen Bridge Blue Hour Photography (拱宸桥) — Quick Photo Stop

Cultural & Historical Context: Gongchen Bridge is the symbolic southern terminus of the UNESCO World Heritage Beijing-Hangzhou Grand Canal, completed in the Ming Dynasty. The massive three-arch stone bridge spans an active working canal with cargo barges and tourist vessels, embodying 1,000 years of ancient Chinese water transport civilization. The adjacent Qiaoxi Historic District preserves old canal warehouse architecture and folk art heritage.

📸 Photo Spot Callout: Blue hour window 17:32–17:42 for lit bridge arch reflections and twilight canal glow; bridge deck southbound canal views; passing barge motion shots. Quick photos only — NO full historic town stroll (duplicates Wuzhen scenery)

18:10–19:00 Dinner near Grand Canal Area

19:00–19:30 Return to Shangri-La to Pick Up Luggage

19:30+ Final Transfer to Holiday Inn Hangzhou Airport Zone

🍽️ Hangzhou Signature Cultural Dishes (Hangbang Cuisine)

Hangzhou (ancient Lin’an) is the home of Hangbang cuisine: light, fresh, balanced sweet-savoury flavours built on lake freshwater, mountain tea and seasonal local produce. These are the essential dishes to try during your stay.

Iconic Main Dishes

  • Dongpo Pork (Dōngpō ròu, 东坡肉) — Legend linked to poet Su Dongpo. Cubed pork belly slow-braised with yellow rice wine and rock sugar; glossy deep red, melt-in-mouth, rich but not greasy, often served with small sweet fermented-rice buns.
  • Longjing Shrimp (Lóngjǐng xiā rén, 龙井虾仁) — Famous tea-infused specialty. Plump river shrimp stir-fried with fresh Longjing tea leaves; clean subtle tea aroma, tender sweet shrimp, light seasoning with no heavy sauce.
  • West Lake Vinegar Fish (Xī Hú cù yú, 西湖醋鱼) — Classic West Lake signature. Gently poached grass carp coated in sweet-sour vinegar sauce; authentic version is not deep-fried, delicate sweet-sour umami. Note: acquired taste for some visitors.
  • Beggar’s Chicken (Jiào huā jī, 叫花鸡) — Chicken wrapped in lotus leaves and sealed in clay, slow baked. Crack open the clay shell at the table; ultra-tender meat infused with lotus-leaf herbal fragrance, served with salt-pepper dip.

Cold Dishes & Soup Classics

  • Hangzhou Braised Duck (Hángzhōu lǔ yā, 杭州卤鸭) — Local cold-cut staple. Duck simmered in soy-spice brine, savoury with gentle sweetness, excellent starter.
  • West Lake Beef Soup (Xī Hú niúròu gēng, 西湖牛肉羹) — Silky smooth soup with minced beef, egg drop and fine starch; mild, comforting and visitor-favourite.

Snacks & Desserts

  • Sweet Lotus-Root with Osmanthus (Guìhuā nuòmǐ ǒu, 桂花糯米藕) — Lotus root stuffed with glutinous rice, drizzled with osmanthus-honey syrup; soft, fragrant, mild sweet dessert.

Recommended Sit-Down Restaurant

印湖·老底子杭帮菜(河坊街店) — Well-reviewed West Lake area restaurant for the full set of classic Hangzhou dishes, balanced flavour and reasonable pricing.

Ordering tip: For 2 people, select 2–3 main dishes + one soup; avoid over-ordering. You may skip West Lake Vinegar Fish if you prefer to avoid the acquired sweet-sour flavour.
