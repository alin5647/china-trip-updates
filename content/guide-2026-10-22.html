11:30 Segment 1: Yongdingmen 永定门 — the south end of the Axis

🎧 Hear your guide — Yongdingmen

Didi Guomao → 永定门城楼： ~30 min, ¥35–45 | Photo stop 12:05–12:15

The gate tower marking the southern start of Beijing's Central Axis (UNESCO World Heritage, 2024). Ten minutes: shoot the tower, then look straight north — on a clear day the axis runs 7.8 km through everything you saw yesterday to the Drum Tower. Then a 5-minute taxi west to the museum.

12:20 Segment 2: Xiannongtan 先农坛 + Beijing Ancient Architecture Museum

🎧 Hear your guide — Xiannongtan & the star ceiling

In the museum 12:20–13:25 | 东经路21号 | Entry ¥15 | Passport required | Open 9:00–17:00

Where Ming and Qing emperors plowed a ceremonial furrow each spring. Go straight to Taisui Hall 太岁殿 for the prize: the “Heavenly Palace” caisson ceiling from Longfu Temple — a Ming-dynasty wooden dome painted with 1,400+ golden stars, arguably the finest ceiling in China. Then the altar terrace with its Five-Color Soil.

13:35 Segment 3: Lunch — Hongyuan Nanmen Hotpot 宏源南门涮肉 (original store)

🎧 Hear your guide — Nanmen mutton hotpot

13:35–14:40 | 永定门东街东里13号楼 (outside Temple of Heaven South Gate) | ~¥100/person

Taxi from the museum ~10 min. Arriving at 13:35 dodges the worst of the lunch queue. After lunch, Didi north to Nanluoguxiang ~35 min (or subway: the hutong has its own Line 6/8 station).

The original store of Beijing's most-loved mutton hotpot chain: clear-broth copper pot, fresh hand-cut mutton, sesame sauce with chive flowers, sesame buns. No English menu — point at the mutton plates. Clear broth is gentle on the stomach; go easy on cabbage and tofu if you're still gassy.

15:20 Segment 4: Nanluoguxiang 南锣鼓巷 → boba + sunglasses at Di'anmen

🎧 Hear your guide — Nanluoguxiang, boba & sunglasses

Hutong 15:20–15:50 | Boba + MINISO 15:50–16:10

Stroll Nanluoguxiang from its south end up to 鼓楼东大街 — Beijing's most famous hutong, subway-served (Line 6/8 南锣鼓巷站）. At the north end, cross to 地安门外大街:

Boba: 茶话弄 （地安门外大街店） — right by Beihai's North Gate and 什刹海 station. Get the 梅占摇红 or 桂花引， the two signatures everyone orders.
Sunglasses for Justin: 名创优品 MINISO （鼓楼东大街店） — same neighborhood, shelves of ¥15–30 non-prescription sunglasses. Five minutes, done.

16:15 Segment 5: Beihai Park 北海公园 — BACK gate in, FRONT gate out

🎧 Hear your guide — Beihai, north to south

Park 16:15–17:05 | Entry ¥10

1. Enter the BACK (North) Gate 北门 from 地安门西大街
2. Walk the north shore west, cross to Qiong Island 琼岛
3. Climb to the White Dagoba 白塔 (1651) — photo terrace over the lake, Forbidden City rooftops beyond
4. Descend the island's south side, cross the Yong'an Bridge
5. Exit the FRONT (South) Gate 南门 by 17:05 — Jingshan is straight ahead across the moat

17:15 Segment 6: Jingshan Park 景山 — SUNSET panorama (core photo stop)

🎧 Hear your guide — Jingshan at sunset

Enter Jingshan South Gate | Summit 17:15–17:50 | Sunset ≈ 17:25 | Entry ¥2

From Beihai's South Gate, walk east along the Forbidden City moat (~10 min) to Jingshan South Gate 景山南门, then straight up to Wanchun Pavilion 万春亭. It's a brisk 10-minute climb — you'll catch the sunset's tail and the blue hour, when the palace roofs go gold, then purple.

The single best panorama in Beijing: dead-center on the Central Axis, the Forbidden City below you, the White Dagoba you just climbed off to the left, Drum Tower straight ahead north. Dark footnote: on the east slope, the last Ming emperor hanged himself in 1644.
