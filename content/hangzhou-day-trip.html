📌 行程概要 Overview

住宿：杭州城中香格里拉｜正餐回酒店吃，路上仅吃小吃｜云栖竹径门票 ¥8/人，西湖湖滨免费
今日日落 17:17，蓝调约 17:40–18:20，18:45 前回酒店 — 原稿的 19:45 蓝调太晚了，那时已全黑
明日安排：飞来峰 & 灵隐寺（已预约早上7:30，不要今天跑过去）

Accommodation: Shangri‑La Midtown Hangzhou. Dinner back at hotel, only street snacks today. Tomorrow morning 7:30 pre‑booked Feilai Peak / Lingyin Temple.

⏰ 13:00‑14:30｜云栖竹径（第一站）Yunqi Bamboo Path
Look for: entrance archway + 陈云 stele 云栖竹径； central bluestone 御道 walked by Kangxi and Qianlong； 洗心亭 pond reflection (get low, pond level)； 回龙亭 frame-within-a-frame. Turn around at Huilong — do not hike deep to Wuyun Mountain. Temple founded 967, rebuilt by Lianchi in the Ming, New Ten Scenes of West Lake 1985.

从酒店乘车前往云栖竹径，清幽山谷竹林，茂密竹林避开正午强光，沿着主石板路游览，不深入五云山，保留体力留给傍晚西湖日落。途中可买小吃垫肚子。

Depart hotel by car to Yunqi Bamboo Path, quiet valley bamboo grove, thick canopy blocks harsh midday sun. Stay on main stone trail, avoid deep hike toward Wuyun Mountain to save strength for sunset at West Lake later. Grab snacks on route.

📸 拍摄要点 Photography Tips

竹林夹道、阳光透过竹叶的斑驳光影；必打卡：洗心亭（池水倒影）、回龙亭（电影感框景）

Shoot winding stone path framed by tall bamboo, dappled light through leaves. Must‑see: Xinxin Pavilion pond reflection, Huilong Pavilion cinematic frame shot.

⏰ 14:30‑15:15｜转场：云栖竹径 → 六公园 Transit to Six Park

坐车返回西湖方向，路上休息补水，想吃零食可以顺路采购，随拍沿途风景，保存主力拍摄精力留给西湖。

Ride back toward West Lake. Rest and hydrate, optional snacks. Casual snapshots only, preserve main shooting energy for lakeside scenery.

⏰ 15:15‑16:15｜六公园，开启白堤漫步 Six Park & Bai Causeway walk
Look for: Six Park water edge first, pagoda across the lake, then walk north to 断桥. Shoot Broken Bridge from the lakeside, not the crowded deck — arch + reflection = full circle. Name from 断桥残雪: sunny side melts first, shady side stays white, bridge looks broken. White Snake legend: Bai Suzhen met Xu Xian here in the rain.

抵达西湖北岸湖滨，相比断桥这里人流量更少，作为环湖步行起点。

Arrive at West Lake north shore, less crowded than Broken Bridge, ideal starting point for lakeside walking.

📸 拍摄要点 Photography Tips

广角湖面远山，垂柳当前景，抓拍湖滨生活化氛围

Wide shot lake + distant hills, use willow branches as foreground, capture casual lakeside atmosphere.

⏰ 16:15‑17:30｜白堤至平湖秋月 · 黄金日落时段 Pinghu Qiuyue Golden Hour
Look for: 平湖秋月 stele, water terrace (the moon-viewing platform), octagonal pavilion + reflection, willow silhouettes, boats crossing gold water. Order: wide (pavilion + sun + lake) → reflection (phone low) → one willow branch black against gold. History: Tang Wanghu Pavilion, Kangxi remodelled it in 1699 as Imperial Book Pavilion — built to look at the moon. Bai Causeway ~1 km, named for governor-poet Bai Juyi; Jindai Bridge midway is the best two-way viewpoint.

光线柔和温暖，全天湖景最佳拍摄窗口，越靠近断桥游客越多。

Light turns warm and soft, best window for lake photos, crowds increase as you near Broken Bridge.

📸 拍摄要点 Photography Tips

湖面金色波光，垂柳剪影；平湖秋月亭台倒影，游船增加画面动感；断桥岸边低角度拍桥拱倒影，避开桥上人群

Golden ripples on water, willow silhouettes; pavilion reflections at Pinghu Qiuyue, boats add movement. Shoot Broken Bridge arch reflection from lakeside to avoid crowd on bridge deck.

⏰ 17:30‑18:30｜北山街 · 黄昏 Beishan Street Dusk
Look for: S-curve road under plane trees, pagoda appearing between branches, and the 10-minute mixed light when lamps are on but the sky is still pink-orange — that is the Beishan shot. Walk east from Gushan across Xiling Bridge (Gushan = West Lake's only natural island; Xiling Seal Society founded 1904, pass by tonight).

北山街老洋房行道树，眺望保俶塔，天空由金黄过渡粉橙色，日落转蓝调。

Historic villas and plane trees along Beishan Street, view toward Baoshu Pagoda. Sky shifts golden to pink‑orange as sunset transitions to blue hour.

📸 拍摄要点 Photography Tips

S形道路树影，远景带上保俶塔；捕捉刚亮起路灯与落日共存的氛围感

S‑curve street with tree shadows, frame Baoshu Pagoda far background; capture mixed sunset glow + newly lit street lamps.

⏰ 18:30‑19:45｜蓝调时刻 · 西湖夜景 Blue Hour Night Scene
Look for: lake turned deep blue, pagoda lit, lamp reflections rippling. Long exposure / night mode, brace on a railing. Name Baochu 保俶 = “protect Chu”, for Qian Chu, last king of Wuyue. End ~18:45, DiDi back to the hotel (~10–15 min) for dinner. Tomorrow 07:30 Feilai Peak / Lingyin — do not go today.

湖水变为深邃蓝色，保俶塔、路灯全部点亮，少量小吃，正餐回酒店享用。

Lake turns deep blue, Baoshu Pagoda and street lamps light up. Light snacks only, dinner back at Shangri‑La.

📸 拍摄要点 Photography Tips

长曝光拍出丝滑湖面；暮色天空下亮灯保俶塔；街灯湖面波光倒影

Long exposure for silky lake surface; lit pagoda against twilight sky, rippling lamp reflections.
