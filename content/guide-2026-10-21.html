08:00 Segment 1: Shangri‑La → Temple of Heaven (South Gate)

🎧 Hear your guide — Temple of Heaven

Target depart hotel: 08:00 | Arrive Tiantan South Gate ~08:40 | Finish sightseeing by 11:10

Subway: Walk ~400m → Wanshisi Station Line16
Line16 → Erligou transfer Line6 → Nanluoguxiang transfer Line8 → Tianqiao Station Exit C
Time: 55–60 min | Fare: ¥5
Didi: Direct to 天坛南门
Time: 40–60 min (traffic variable) | Cost: ¥55–70

Context: Built 1420 Ming Yongle era, same time as Forbidden City. Imperial altar for heaven worship and harvest prayers. Blue roof tiles = heaven; circles = heaven, squares = earth. Enter South Gate to follow the original imperial procession route.

Tiantan Internal Walking Route (South → North)

1. South Gate → Circular Mound Altar (Yuanqiu Tan) 圜丘坛
  • Open-air three-tier round stone sacrificial platform; no roof
  • All stone rings use 9 or multiples of 9 (sacred imperial number)
  • Center “Heart of Heaven Stone”: soft speech bounces back via acoustic design
2. North 200m → Imperial Vault of Heaven (Huangqiongyu) 皇穹宇
  • Holds spirit tablets for heaven, sun, moon, stars, weather gods
  • Echo Wall: whisper along curved brick wall to someone far away
  • Triple Sound Stone (in front of hall): clap once, hear three echoes
3. Danbi Bridge 丹陛桥 (Sacred Path)
  • Middle lane: only heaven’s tablet, humans forbidden
  • Right lane: Emperor only; Left lane: ministers
4. Prayer for Grain Hall (Qiniandian) 祈年殿
  • Iconic round hall, 28 massive wooden pillars, no original nails
  • 4 center pillars = 4 seasons; 12 middle = months; 12 outer = traditional 2-hour time blocks
  • Burned 1889, rebuilt; beautiful dougong bracket woodwork inside

Exit via East Gate (closest to Tianqiao subway for next leg)

11:10 Segment 2: Tiantan East Gate → Qianmen / Dashilan / Yangmeizhu Xiejie / Liulichang

🎧 Hear your guide — Qianmen, Dashilan & the hutongs

Depart Tiantan:11:10 | Arrive Dashilan ~11:30 | Max hutong time:1.5h | Leave Qianmen by 13:30

Subway: Tianqiao Line8 north 1 stop → Qianmen Station Exit B
Time: ~10 min | Fare: ¥3
Didi: Dashilan West Entrance 大栅栏西口
Time: ~10 min | Cost: ¥15–20

Context: Qianmen = old Beijing inner city south gate. Dashilan: 500-year-old historic commercial street. Yangmeizhu Xiejie = slanted hutong (“xiejie”) following an old water channel, home to writers and artists in early 1900s. Liulichang was scholar/exam district for brush, ink, paper, seals.

Hutong Walking Route

1. Qianmen South Entrance → skip busy main tourist strip, head west into Dashilan
  • Look for century brands: Tongrentang herbal medicine, Ruifuxiang silk, Neiliansheng cloth shoes
2. Dashilan northwest → turn onto Yangmeizhu Xiejie 杨梅竹斜街 (main highlight)
  • Grey brick siheyuan courtyards, old wooden door knockers
  • Small independent bookstores, letterpress studio, tiny galleries, quiet coffee shops
  • Lunch rule: quick snack only, no long sit-down meal
3. End of Yangmeizhu → 8 min walk → Liulichang East Street 琉璃厂东街
  • Brush, ink sticks, rice paper, ink slabs, scrolls, seal carving workshops
  • Originally a glazed tile kiln for palace construction (“liuli” = colored glaze)
4. Liulichang → 9 min walk → Qianmen North Entrance, near Qianmen subway station

13:30 Segment3: Qianmen → 798 Art Zone (South Gate)

🎧 Hear your guide — 798 Art Zone

Depart Qianmen:13:30 | Arrive ~15:30 | Visit window:15:30–17:40

Subway: Qianmen Line2 east → Jinsong transfer Line14 northeast → Jiangtai Station Exit B, walk 10 min to 798 South Gate
Time: ~60 min | Fare: ¥5
Didi: 798 South Gate
Time:50–70 min (afternoon traffic risk) | Cost:¥70–90

Admission: 798 grounds FREE. Individual special galleries ¥10–50 optional. Most indoor galleries start closing ~17:30, prioritize outdoor art first.

Context: Built 1952 by East German engineers as state electronics factory. Closed in 90s; cheap warehouse space attracted China’s avant-garde artists. Industrial cranes, smokestacks, arched factory windows mixed with contemporary art.

798 Loop Route (start South Gate)

1. Enter South Gate, north along main road → find iconic red 798 wall sign (first photo stop)
2. Left to 751 D-Park (free, adjacent)
  • Giant old steam locomotive, huge gas holder tank, industrial pipelines, street murals
3. West along warehouse galleries
  • UCCA Center: huge contemporary art space; only lobby peek today to save time
  • Watch for original factory details: rusted cranes, numbered concrete doors, German arched windows
4. South back to main sculpture plaza, then return toward South Gate

Quick snack: Zui Mian 醉面, ~¥20/person simple noodles near south entrance if hungry before dinner. Exit 798 no later than 17:40

General Day Notes

  • Tiantan morning: locals fly pet songbirds in bamboo cages, classic Beijing hobby
  • Yangmeizhu: siheyuan are private homes; do not walk inside courtyards
  • 798: open artist studios — quiet hello is polite; don’t wander uninvited
  • Qianmen rickshaw drivers will approach: overpriced, politely decline
  • Wear good walking shoes, you will walk many kilometers
