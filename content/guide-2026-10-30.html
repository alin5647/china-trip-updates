Audio tour — Stop 4: Dragon's Back hike + Shek O (Oct 30, 7:30 AM)

🎧 Hear your guide, with walk music underneath — Dragon's Back
📌 Your itinerary slot (revised Oct 29): Oct 30 — breakfast first: Garden Cafe at the Conrad (included, opens 6:30 AM), out by ~7:05 (Lin Heung dropped — hotel breakfast every morning is the new default). Getting there: first, five minutes at the Admiralty station customer service centre (under the hotel) — two On-Loan Octopus cards, HK$150 each (HK$50 refundable deposit + HK$100 loaded; one card per person, no sharing; deposit and leftover balance come back at the airport on Sunday). Then MTR Admiralty → Shau Kei Wan (Exit A3), Citybus 9 to To Tei Wan — the stop is at the trailhead. On the trail ~8:00 AM: Dragon's Back (龙脊, Lóng Jǐ), ~5–6 km / 2–2.5 hours over Shek O Peak (284 m) and along the ridge, descending into Shek O. Lunch in Shek O village. Forecast: mostly sunny, high 29°C, UV very high — carry 1.5 L of water each; nothing on the ridge.
Look for: the famous ridge trail, sea views down to Shek O beach. This is also your best candidate area to safely test the DJI Flip — check the HK CAD drone map first, never fly over crowds.

Afternoon — Tai Kwun + PMQ + the Central escalators (Oct 30, ~2:45–5:30 PM, added Oct 29)
